Mezgeb

Digital Bridge Accessibility Hackathon 2026 · Track 1: Accessible onboarding

Accessible sign-up any website can add with one script tag

Mezgeb is a sign-up module that asks one clear question per screen, with voice or typing and help that never sees what you typed. It is for learners who use a screen reader, cannot read well, have no ID document yet, or are on a slow phone. It works in Amharic and English.

Add it to your site

Build mezgeb.js on your Mezgeb server, register a publishable site key, then add two lines to your sign-up page.

<script type="module" src="https://YOUR-MEZGEB-SERVER/v1/mezgeb.js"></script>
<mezgeb-register site-key="pk_test_mysite" lang="am" platform-name="My site"></mezgeb-register>
